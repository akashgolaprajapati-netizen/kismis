My dearest Twinkle, I wanted to do something a little different for our first anniversary. One whole year. It feels like just yesterday we were starting this journey, and at the exact same time, it feels like my soul has known you for a lifetime.
But before I get too deeply romantic, I need to start with an apology. I know that lately, I haven't been giving you the time and attention that you truly deserve. Life gets chaotic and schedules get busy, but that is never an excuse to make you feel like you aren't my absolute priority. I am so deeply sorry for the moments my mind was distracted when it should have been solely on you. You deserve the world, and I promise to be better, to be present, and to give you all the time you deserve.

So, do you forgive me?

Now that we have that out of the way... can we talk about how illegally beautiful you are? Seriously, it is genuinely unfair to the rest of the population. Sometimes I catch myself just staring at you, wondering how I pulled off the biggest heist of the century by getting you to be mine.
You have this effortless glow, and when you smile, it completely disarms me. If being that gorgeous was a crime, you would be serving a life sentence without parole. Every time I see you, I feel that exact same flutter in my chest that I felt on day one.
And honestly? I love you even more than a massive plate of Afghani Tandoori Chaap or perfectly made Kadhi Chawal... and you know exactly how deep my love for food goes, so that is saying a lot. You are probably the only person on this planet I would gladly share my paneer with.
But your beauty goes so much deeper than what I can see. I am in absolute awe of your spirit. The way you navigate the world with such kindness, grace, and an undeniable spark is intoxicating. You bring a special kind of energy to everything you touch, and you light up my life in ways I never thought possible.
It’s in the little things, too. The way you laugh, the way you look at me when you think I’m being ridiculous, and the way your presence instantly calms my mind. You are my safe space, my best friend, and my greatest adventure all rolled into one.
When I look back at the past twelve months, my heart feels like it could burst. Thank you for the greatest memories of my life. I think about the afternoons we spent hopping between cafes and hitting up street food spots in Delhi, talking for hours and completely losing track of time in each other's company.
I think about our movie dates. Sitting in the dark theater, sharing snacks, whispering jokes to each other, and honestly paying way more attention to your reactions than whatever blockbuster was playing on the screen. Those simple moments mean everything to me.
Then there is the sheer energy of the outdoor music concerts and festivals we went to. Feeling the bass, being surrounded by the massive crowds, but feeling like we were in our own little world. The only thing I was truly focused on was holding your hand and watching you smile under the lights.
I also love our little cultural adventures—walking through museums, taking in the history and the artifacts. The exhibits were fascinating, I will admit, but honestly, I was mostly just looking at you. As far as I'm concerned, you are the real masterpiece.
And I will never, ever forget that day boating on the lake. The water, the breeze, and just you and me. It is a picture painted in my mind forever. Every single flirty glance and deep midnight conversation has built this beautiful, unshakable foundation that we stand on today.
You make me want to be the absolute best version of myself. You make me want to dress better, work harder, and be a man who is truly worthy of standing by your side. Watching you live your life and chase your happiness is the greatest privilege I could ever ask for.
Thank you, Twinkle. Thank you for loving me, for being patient with me, and for letting me be yours. This past year was incredible, but it is just the warm-up. I cannot wait for all the trouble we are going to get into next.
Happy 1 Year Anniversary.
Forever obsessed with you,
